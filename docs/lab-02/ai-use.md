\# Lab 2 AI Use and Reflection



\## LLM Used



I used ChatGPT (GPT-5.6 Luna) to support the development, testing, troubleshooting, and documentation of TokTickIT Lab 2.

\## My Reflection



AI helped me understand the Lab 2 requirements, troubleshoot development problems, review my implementation, and organize my testing and submission evidence. It was useful for suggesting solutions and explaining technical problems, but I still needed to implement, test, and verify the changes myself. This helped me use AI as a development assistant rather than relying on it to complete the work without verification.
